# 媒体解锁卡图像生成记录

生成模式：OpenAI 内置 ImageGen，`stylized-concept`。两张图均采用纯色绿幕背景生成，再通过软蒙版、去绿溢色和 384×384 RGBA 缩放得到应用内轻量资产。

## 图片解锁卡

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: square iOS game inventory item icon for an image unlock card
Primary request: Create one premium collectible IMAGE UNLOCK CARD item, instantly readable at 48–96 pt.
Subject: a single rounded rectangular magical ticket/card in a slight three-quarter front view, with a bold raised photo-landscape glyph (mountain and sun) combined with a small gold open padlock/keyhole motif; add one subtle cat-paw seal as the app-world cue.
Style/medium: polished high-end 3D game item render, rounded toy-like proportions, glossy enamel and satin metal, visually compatible with cute premium purple-and-gold gift assets.
Composition/framing: one isolated object centered, occupying about 72% of a square canvas, generous clean padding, strong simple silhouette, no floor.
Lighting/mood: soft studio rim light, luminous and magical but not noisy.
Color palette: lavender, periwinkle, icy cyan, pearl white, restrained warm gold trim. Do not use green in the object.
Materials/textures: compact glossy enamel, satin gold, a few broad highlights; avoid tiny details.
Scene/backdrop: perfectly flat solid #00ff00 chroma-key background for background removal; one uniform color, no shadows, gradients, texture, reflections, floor plane, or lighting variation.
Constraints: no words, no letters, no numbers, no watermark, no border around the canvas, no cast shadow, no contact shadow, no reflection; crisp separated edges; the photo glyph and open-lock meaning must remain obvious when downscaled.
Avoid: extra cards, hands, characters, scenery, photorealistic paper texture, green spill, thin filigree, clutter.
```
